How it works

Two drops,one pool.

Merge is Merge's rescue cousin. It isn't two equals combining: a smaller, drying coin flows into a stronger one, with protections for the host.

  1. 1
    Drop in

    Anyone proposes that a drying coin flow into a stronger one. The terms are computed from live market data.

  2. 2
    Ripple

    Holders of both coins vote for 24 hours, weighted by on-chain token balance. Both sides need a majority and a quorum.

  3. 3
    Merge

    The target's liquidity pours into the host's pool. Target holders swap into the host at the discounted ratio.

  4. 4
    Settle

    The target coin is retired and its page reads "Mergeed into $HOST". The community keeps its identity and its water.

Swap discountTarget holders receive host tokens below the fair ratio, so existing host holders aren't diluted at full price.
VestingOnly a quarter of Mergeed tokens unlock at execution. The rest trickle out linearly, so they can't all flood the host's pool on day one.
Size gateA target can't exceed half of the host's market cap, so a host is never swallowed by something bigger.
!
Be honest about the currents. Someone could buy a dried-up coin cheap and push an absorption to dump on host holders. The discount, vesting, size gate and pre-proposal balance snapshots are the defenses. An absorption is a migration, not a guarantee the new tokens hold value.
